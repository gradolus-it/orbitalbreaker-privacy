Orbital Breaker - Deleting your online data

Developer: Wbison, contact: gradolusit@gmail.com
See also the privacy policy.

What the online leaderboards keep

Only the online leaderboards send anything from the game. For them the server keeps:

  • a random player ID and a hash of its secret;
  • the pilot name: a generated one such as "NOVA HAWK 42", or the one you picked;
  • the country shown next to the name, if any;
  • your best score for each daily and weekly challenge, with the replay of that run;
  • names you reported, with the time.

There is no account, email address or device ID. Your progress, settings and records on the device never leave it.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Options > About.
  2. Select Online boards: On. The row asks "Delete online data?".
  3. Select it again within a few seconds.

The game deletes your player, all your entries, all your replays and your reports from the server, then shows "Online data deleted". The online boards stay off, also after a restart, until you turn them on again. Without a connection nothing is deleted; the game keeps your ID so you can try again.

Deleted automatically

Daily boards and their replays are deleted after 30 days, weekly boards after 12 weeks, and a player with no entries left is deleted with them. Even if you do nothing, nothing stays on the server longer than 12 weeks after your last run. Copies in the server's daily backups are removed within 14 days after that.

If you no longer have the game

The player ID exists only on your device, so after uninstalling the game nobody can tell which entries were yours. They are deleted automatically as described above. To have them deleted sooner, write to gradolusit@gmail.com with your pilot name and the day of the run, and the developer deletes the matching entries.

Orbital Breaker - Brisanje spletnih podatkov

Razvijalec: Wbison, stik: gradolusit@gmail.com
Glej tudi politiko zasebnosti.

Kaj hranijo spletne lestvice

Iz igre pošiljajo podatke samo spletne lestvice. Zanje strežnik hrani:

  • naključni ID igralca in zgoščeno vrednost njegove skrivnosti;
  • ime pilota: ustvarjeno, na primer »NOVA HAWK 42«, ali tisto, ki si ga izbral;
  • državo ob imenu, če je prikazana;
  • tvoj najboljši rezultat za vsak dnevni in tedenski izziv s posnetkom te igre;
  • imena, ki si jih prijavil, s časom prijave.

Računa, e-poštnega naslova ali ID-ja naprave ni. Napredek, nastavitve in rekordi v napravi je nikoli ne zapustijo.

Izbris v igri

  1. Odpri Nastavitve > O igri.
  2. Izberi Spletne lestvice: vklop. Vrstica vpraša »Izbrišem spletne podatke?«.
  3. V nekaj sekundah jo izberi še enkrat.

Igra izbriše tvojega igralca, vse tvoje vnose, posnetke in prijave s strežnika in pokaže »Spletni podatki izbrisani«. Spletne lestvice ostanejo izklopljene, tudi po ponovnem zagonu, dokler jih znova ne vklopiš. Brez povezave se nič ne izbriše; igra obdrži tvoj ID, da lahko poskusiš znova.

Samodejni izbris

Dnevne lestvice in njihovi posnetki se izbrišejo po 30 dneh, tedenske po 12 tednih, igralec brez vnosov pa skupaj z njimi. Tudi če ne narediš ničesar, na strežniku nič ne ostane dlje kot 12 tednov po tvoji zadnji igri. Kopije v dnevnih varnostnih kopijah strežnika izginejo v 14 dneh za tem.

Če igre nimaš več

ID igralca obstaja samo v tvoji napravi, zato po odstranitvi igre nihče ne more ugotoviti, kateri vnosi so bili tvoji. Izbrišejo se samodejno, kot je opisano zgoraj. Če želiš, da se izbrišejo prej, piši na gradolusit@gmail.com z imenom pilota in dnevom igre, in razvijalec izbriše ustrezne vnose.
