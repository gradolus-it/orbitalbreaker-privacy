Orbital Breaker - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Developer: Wbison, contact: gradolusit@gmail.com

Orbital Breaker does not sell personal data. It sends data to the developer only if you turn on online leaderboards (see below).

What the app stores

The game saves your progress and settings on your device only:

  • level, score, lives, high score, stars per level and unlocked levels;
  • difficulty, game mode, craft color, audio, control and accessibility settings;
  • achievements, discovered brick types, run history and local records;
  • your best ghost run and the replays of your best challenge runs;
  • a local error log that helps the developer when you report a problem. It stays on your device unless you send it yourself.

This data never leaves your device through the game; only the optional online leaderboards described below send anything. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Device backup

If you use Android's device backup, Android may include the game's save files in your personal backup, managed by Google under your Google account settings. The developer has no access to these backups.

Sensors

If you choose tilt controls, the game reads the accelerometer only while you play, to move the paddle. Sensor data is not stored or sent anywhere.

Network

On Android the game uses the network only for Google Play (the in-app purchase, see below) and, if you turn them on, for the online leaderboards.

Online leaderboards (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Online boards" in the options, the game creates a random player ID, a random secret and a generated name such as "NOVA HAWK 42" on your device. When you finish a daily or weekly challenge with a new personal best, the game sends to the developer's leaderboard service over an encrypted connection:

  • that player ID, the secret and the name;
  • the recorded inputs of the run (a replay), which the service plays again to compute your score.

The service stores the ID, a hash of the secret (never the secret itself), the name, your best score per challenge and its replay. Boards and replays are deleted automatically after 30 days (daily) or 12 weeks (weekly). Turning "Online boards" off deletes your player, your entries and your replays from the service. The service also processes your IP address to limit request rates; it is not stored with your entries. The leaderboard service is not running yet. Its hosting provider and location will be named here before it starts.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The game has no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The online leaderboard identity is a random ID, not an account with personal details.

In-app purchase

The game is free to try and offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. Google Play handles the purchase, promo codes and your payment details; Google's privacy policy applies to it. The game asks Google Play only whether the unlock is owned and stores the answer on your device. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
